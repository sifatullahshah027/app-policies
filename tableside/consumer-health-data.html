Tableside Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · For residents of Washington, Nevada and other US states with consumer health data laws

This page supplements the Tableside Privacy Policy. It describes the “consumer health data” that Tableside handles, as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws. “We” means the developer of Tableside, reachable at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

1. Consumer health data we collect

  • Meals you log, with calorie and macronutrient figures, and the dishes and restaurant names from menu readings;
  • Body measurements and goals: weight and weigh-ins, height, age, sex, weight goal, activity level, and calorie and macronutrient targets;
  • Dietary choices and foods you avoid (which can reveal allergies or intolerances you choose to enter);
  • Photos of plates attached to meals you log.

2. Why we collect it

Only to provide the app you asked for: to estimate what you eat, set and track your targets, show your history and progress, and, if you sign in, back it up so it can be restored on another phone. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Where it comes from

Directly from you, when you answer setup questions, log meals and weigh-ins, or photograph a plate. Calorie estimates are produced by the AI reading of your photo.

4. Where it is kept

On your device. If, and only if, you sign in, it is also stored under your account on Google Firebase so it can be restored. Photos sent for reading are read and discarded, not stored.

5. Sharing

We do not sell consumer health data and we do not share it with third parties or affiliates. It is processed only by our service providers, on our behalf and under contract:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): stores your backup if you sign in.
  • Anthropic and Google (Gemini API): receive a plate or menu photo for the moment it takes to read it. No other health data is sent to them.

Health figures are never sent to analytics.

6. Your rights

You can confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, see it, have it deleted, and withdraw your consent to its collection:

  • See and download it: in the app, You → Download my data.
  • Delete it and withdraw consent: in the app, You → Delete my data → Delete everything, as shown on the account deletion page.
  • By email: write to sifatullahshah27@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We reply within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with “Appeal” in the subject; we answer appeals within 45 days, and if you are not satisfied you may contact your state Attorney General.
