Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tableside. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tableside. You keep Premium until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

I bought Premium but the app doesn't show it

In Tableside, tap You → Restore purchases, or Restore at the bottom of the paywall. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I move my meals to a new phone?

On the old phone, tap You → Back up with an account and sign in with Apple or Google. Install Tableside on the new phone and sign in with the same account; your history, targets and photos come back. Without an account, your data stays on the old phone only.

Where do my photos go?

When you tap Read, the photo is sent to our server and read by an AI (Anthropic's Claude, or Google's Gemini as a backup), then discarded. Only meal photos you log are kept, and only in your own account if you signed in. See the privacy policy.

How accurate are the calories?

They are AI estimates from the menu's wording or a photo of the plate, and portions vary by kitchen. Use them as a guide, not medical advice. Tableside does not detect allergens: always check with the restaurant.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: You → Delete my data → Delete everything. Step-by-step instructions with screenshots, and how to ask by email, are on the account deletion page.

Can I download my data?

Yes: You → Download my data creates a spreadsheet of your meals and a full JSON copy, which you can save or share.
