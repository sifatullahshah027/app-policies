Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Perfin. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Perfin. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open Settings in Perfin and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

The app can't use my camera

Allow camera access for Perfin in your device's settings. You can also identify a stamp from a photo you have already taken.

Are the values appraisals?

No. Values are AI estimates of a typical copy's range at each condition grade. They are not appraisals or offers to buy.
