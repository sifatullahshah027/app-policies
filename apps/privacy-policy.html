Privacy Policy for Our Apps

Effective 6 November 2026 · Applies to our apps that link to this page, on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

This policy explains what data our apps handle, who receives it and why, and the choices you have. It covers the iOS and Android versions of each app that links to this page. They work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the developer of these apps. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

Not every app uses every feature described here. For example, an app without ads sends nothing to ad networks, and an app without a location feature never asks for your location. Each part of this policy applies only when an app uses that feature. An app with its own privacy policy follows that policy instead.

The short version
  • Many of our apps are free and supported by ads from Google AdMob and its partner networks, including Meta Audience Network.
  • The ad networks, analytics and measurement tools receive your advertising ID, device information, IP address (and the approximate location it shows), and how you use the app and its ads.
  • Ads are personalised only if you allow it. You choose through Apple's tracking prompt on iOS, the consent form in Europe, the UK and Switzerland, and the ad settings on Android.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text, photo or audio you submit is sent to an AI provider to create the answer. Nothing else about you is sent with it.
  • Accounts are optional where an app offers them. If you sign in, we store your email address and the data you choose to back up. You can delete your account from inside the app.
  • We ask for location only in apps with a feature that needs it, and only with your permission.
  • We do not sell your data for money. Sharing data with ad networks for targeted ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws. You can opt out (see section 11).

1. Data we collect and why

Information you give us

  • Support emails: your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you.
  • Information you type or add in an app: for example a name, profile details, notes, preferences, or answers to setup questions. This stays on your device unless the app backs it up to your account (section 2) or you send it to an AI feature (section 3).
  • Feedback and ratings you send from inside an app.

Please do not send us sensitive information, such as health details, religious or political views, or identity documents, unless an app clearly asks for it for a feature you choose to use.

Information collected automatically

Our apps include software development kits (SDKs) from other companies. An SDK is a ready-made code library. The SDKs listed in section 6 collect the following when you use an app:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the Google advertising ID on Android), the identifier for vendors (IDFV) on iOS, and app-instance or installation IDs created by Firebase and other SDKs.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, mobile carrier, network type and app version.
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (city, region or country) is estimated.
  • App interactions: app opens, screens viewed, features used, session length, and in-app events such as a purchase or a completed task.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, viewed or tapped, and whether an app was installed from one of our ads (install attribution).
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error logs and performance data.

Location

  • Approximate location from your IP address is estimated by the ad networks and analytics tools in every app that uses them. No permission is needed for this.
  • Device location (with your permission): some apps have a feature that needs your location, for example local weather, prayer times, nearby places or maps. These apps ask for permission first. Where possible they ask only for approximate location. They ask for precise location only when the feature cannot work without it. The location is used for that feature, on your device or sent to the service that provides the result (for example a weather or maps service). We do not give your device location to ad networks, and we do not keep a history of where you have been. Our apps do not use location in the background unless a feature clearly says so and you allow it.

You can turn location off for any app at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card or billing details. The app receives the status of your purchase from the store. Some apps send the store's purchase token or receipt to our server, or to a subscription service listed in section 6, to check that the purchase is genuine and to restore it on your other devices.

Purchase history: for each purchase, the app records the product or plan you bought, its price and currency, the date, and your subscription status (active, trial, renewed, cancelled or expired). Firebase Analytics records this automatically as an in-app purchase event, to count sales and see which features people pay for. If you sign in, your subscription status can also be saved with your account, so it works on your other devices. We do not use your purchase history for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, an app can send reminders and updates. Some reminders are created on your device. Others are push notifications sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives the app a push token. We use the token only to deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.

2. If you create an account or sign in (optional)

Some apps let you sign in with Apple, Google or an email address, through Firebase Authentication. Signing in is optional unless the app says a feature needs it. When you sign in, we store:

  • Account identity: your email address, an account ID, and the name and profile photo link your Apple or Google account shares with us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's private relay address.
  • Data you choose to back up or sync: for example your settings, history, saved items and content you create in the app, so you can restore it on a new device.

Delete your account: every app with accounts has a delete option inside the app, usually in Settings. Deleting your account deletes the account and the data stored with it on our servers. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

3. AI features

Some apps have features that use a large language model (LLM) or another AI model, for example to answer a question, identify something in a photo, write or translate text, or transcribe audio.

  • What is sent: only the content you submit for that feature (text, a photo, or audio) and the instructions the app adds, such as your app language. Nothing is sent until you use the feature.
  • How it is sent: over an encrypted connection, usually through our server, to an AI provider: OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude), depending on the app and the feature. They are listed in section 6.
  • What is not sent: your name, email address, account ID, advertising ID or device location, unless you type them into your request yourself.
  • Storage: we do not use your submissions to train AI models. Under their paid API terms, the AI providers we use do not use API data to train their models. They keep it only for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, then delete it. Where an app saves your results or history, it saves them on your device, or in your account if you sign in.
  • Accuracy: AI answers can be wrong. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions.

Please do not submit sensitive information, such as health, financial or identity documents, or photos of other people, to an AI feature.

4. Data that stays on your device

Unless an app says otherwise in this policy, content you create in it (for example your history, saved items, notes, photos and settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, under your Apple or Google account.

7. Advertising and your choices

Ads are served by the networks in section 6, not by us. We do not see or store the data they collect. To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, they receive the advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interactions described in section 1. We never give them your name, email address, account data, device location, photos, or anything you type or submit to an AI feature.

Ads are personalised (based on your activity in other apps and websites) only where you allow it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads. These use only the current app, your device and your approximate location, and still use some data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

  • How the choices are shown: our apps use Google's User Messaging Platform, set up in AdMob's Privacy & messaging settings, to show the privacy messages below. Which messages you see depends on your region and device.
  • iPhone and iPad: before the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used, the app may first show a short screen that explains why it asks. This screen has only a Continue button. Then the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, where you choose "Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track". If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared, and Meta and other SDKs are told that tracking is not allowed. Your choice does not change what you can do in the app. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices Settings > Google > Ads). After you delete it, apps get no advertising ID.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open an app with ads, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choice. It is a Google-certified consent management platform that uses the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2. No ads are requested before you choose, and nothing is used for personalised ads unless you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: in these states, the app shows a message from Google's User Messaging Platform with the choice to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted ads. You can open it again at any time in Settings > Privacy options. See section 11.
  • Meta ad settings: if you have a Facebook or Instagram account, you can also control how Meta uses data for ads at facebook.com/adpreferences.

Ads in our apps do not cover content or controls, always have a close button, and full-screen ads appear only at natural breaks.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information for money or rent it to anyone.
  • We do not give your device location, account data, photos or AI submissions to ad networks.
  • We do not use your content or AI submissions to train AI models.
  • We do not read your contacts, calendar, messages or call logs.
  • We do not make you allow tracking or personalised ads to use any feature.

10. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent: personalised ads, reading or storing the advertising identifier on your device, Meta app events, device location, camera, microphone and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy options or in your device settings. This does not affect what happened before.
  • Performing the service you ask for: running an AI feature you use, keeping your account and backup, and checking purchases.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised ads that keep the apps free, fraud prevention, security, crash reports and usage statistics that help us fix and improve the apps.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records and answering lawful requests.

11. US state privacy rights (California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and others)

In the last 12 months, our apps collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (advertising ID, device and installation IDs, IP address, and email address if you sign in), internet or other electronic activity (app and ad interactions), approximate geolocation, precise geolocation only in apps with a location feature you allowed, and content you submit to AI features. We disclosed them to the companies in section 6 for the purposes in sections 1 to 7.

We do not sell personal information for money. But sharing identifiers and ad interactions with ad networks and Meta for targeted (cross-context behavioural) advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws. To opt out, use Settings > Privacy options in the app, turn off tracking on iOS, or delete your advertising ID on Android. We honour Global Privacy Control signals where the platform passes them to us. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. Precise location is used only to provide the feature you asked for.

You also have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and the right not to be treated differently for using these rights. To make a request, email us. If you do not have an account, we may need your advertising ID or another device ID to find your data. Much of the data is held by the companies in section 6, and you can also contact them directly.

12. Your rights (GDPR and UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, email sifatullahshah27@gmail.com. We reply within one month. Many choices are quickest through the steps in section 7, or the delete option in the app.

13. Children

These apps are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We tell the ad networks to treat users identified as under age as child-directed, so they get no personalised ads. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

14. International transfers

The companies in section 6 mainly process data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, they use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where they take part, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

15. Security

Data sent by our apps uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our servers accept requests only from genuine copies of our apps, and account data needs a valid sign-in. AI provider keys are kept on our servers and never ship in the apps. No method of transfer or storage is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to this policy

If we change how our apps handle data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships. We will describe important changes in the app or its release notes.
