Support for Our Apps

Help for our apps on iPhone, iPad and Android

Email sifatullahshah27@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include the app name, your device model, and the app version (usually shown in the app's Settings). For a problem, tell us what you did and what happened.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > choose the app > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > choose the app > Cancel subscription.

You keep the paid features until the end of the period you have paid for.

I paid, but the app does not show it

Open the app's Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account that you bought with. If it still does not work, email us with the order number from your store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us.

  • App Store: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: go to Google Play refund help.

Ads and privacy choices

How do I change my ad or consent choices?

In apps with ads, open the app's Settings and tap Privacy options. This opens the consent form again, so you can change your choice.

How do I stop personalised ads?

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Turn tracking off for the app, or for all apps.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices Settings > Google > Ads). Delete your advertising ID.
  • US residents: use Privacy options in the app to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for targeted ads.

You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. Every feature keeps working.

An ad is wrong or offensive

Email us with the app name, a screenshot of the ad and the time you saw it. We can block it.

Permissions

The app cannot use my camera, photos, microphone or location

Allow the permission in your device settings.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings > choose the app > turn on the permission.
  • Android: Settings > Apps > choose the app > Permissions.

You can turn a permission off again at any time. The rest of the app keeps working.

How do I turn notifications off?

Turn them off in the app's Settings, if it has that option, or in your device settings, under Notifications.

AI features

Are AI answers always correct?

No. AI answers can be wrong. Do not use them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. If you see a bad answer, email us with a screenshot.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

In apps with accounts, open the app's Settings and choose the delete account option. This deletes your account and the data stored with it on our servers. You can also email us from the address you signed in with, and ask us to delete it. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store first (see above).

How do I delete the data on my device?

Delete the app. This deletes the data the app keeps on your device.

How do I see or delete the data you hold?

Email us with the app name. If you have an account, send the email from the address you signed in with. See the Privacy Policy for your rights.
