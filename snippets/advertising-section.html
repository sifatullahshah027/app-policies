What the ad networks collect

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the networks receive:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which they use to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.

{{APP_NAME}} does not give the ad networks your name, email address, photos, precise location or anything you type into the app.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location.

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for your permission to track, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, before the advertising identifier is used. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. Nothing is used for personalised ads until you choose. You can change your choice at any time from {{APP_NAME}} → Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), sharing data with ad networks for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals where the platform passes them to us.

Children

{{APP_NAME}} is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we ask the ad networks not to show personalised ads to anyone identified as a child.

How long ad data is kept

Each ad network keeps the data it collects under its own retention policy, linked above. We do not receive or keep it.
