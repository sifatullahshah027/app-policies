RPM Meter Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Applies to RPM Meter - Stroboscope on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

RPM Meter measures how fast something turns without touching it. It uses your phone's flashlight as a strobe light (Strobe Light Mode) or its camera (Camera Mode). This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of RPM Meter. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your readings and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • The camera image is analysed live on your device. The app never records, saves or uploads photos or video, and it never uses the microphone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob receives device and advertising data, described in section 3. Pro subscribers see no ads.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data that stays on your device

RPM Meter works offline. The following is stored only in the app's storage on your phone or tablet, and we cannot see it:

  • your saved readings: the speed (RPM and Hz), the mode used, the date and time, the machine name and type you choose, any note you type, and the strobe or camera settings used for that reading;
  • your settings, such as appearance, haptics, the default mode and your flash and camera preferences;
  • a technical profile of your phone that sets a safe strobe speed: the camera hardware level, the amount of memory, the fastest rate your flashlight handled, and a marker written while the strobe runs so the app can lower the limit if the phone restarted mid-strobe (Android).

This data leaves your device only when you choose to send it:

  • CSV export (Pro). When you export readings, the file goes to the app or location you pick in the share sheet. That destination's own privacy terms apply.
  • Emails to support. When you tap Contact support or Report a problem, your email app opens with the app version, build, plan (Free or Pro), platform and a short hardware summary filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.
  • Your device's own backup. The app's storage may be included in your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) and in Android's backup to your Google account, which Apple or Google run under your account.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data from the device.

3. Advertising

The free version of RPM Meter is supported by advertising. Ads appear on the Machines and Tools screens, and occasionally as a full-screen ad after you save a reading. No full-screen ad is shown while a measurement is running: one that comes due then waits until the measurement ends. If you choose to, you can watch a rewarded ad to unlock fine adjustment for 10 minutes. Subscribers to RPM Meter Pro see no ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, not by us. We do not see or store the data AdMob collects.

What the ad network collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google receives:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which Google uses to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostics about the ad software, such as load times and errors.

RPM Meter does not give Google your readings, machine names, notes, camera images, precise location or anything you type into the app.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location. We ask Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences.

  • iPhone and iPad: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If the app does not ask, or you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. Nothing is used for personalised ads until you choose. You can change your choice at any time from RPM Meter → Settings → Privacy choices.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), sharing data with an ad network for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy choices in the app where it is shown, through your device's tracking or ad settings above, or by emailing us. We honour Global Privacy Control signals where the platform passes them to us.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

RPM Meter Pro subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card or billing details. The app reads the status of your subscription from the store on your device, and keeps that status on your device. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to your purchase. If you rate the app, the rating prompt is shown by your phone's operating system.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run a server for RPM Meter or keep a copy of your readings.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or any data service other than the ad network above.
  • We do not record, store or upload camera images, and we never use the microphone.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information. The only sharing for advertising is the data Google AdMob collects as described in section 3, which you can limit or turn off.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email sifatullahshah27@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Your readings are on your device, so you control them directly: you can edit or delete any reading in the app, export them (Pro), or uninstall the app. We do not hold advertising data ourselves. For data Google holds, use the choices in section 3 or Google's own tools.

Where the GDPR applies, personalised ads rely on your consent, given in the consent form and withdrawable at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in funding the free version. Replying to support emails relies on our legitimate interest in helping you.

9. Children

RPM Meter is a measuring tool for mechanics, engineers and hobbyists and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we ask Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your readings are protected by your device's own security. Ad and store traffic uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, so keep your device's screen lock on.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
