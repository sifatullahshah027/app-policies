2. Safety

  • Flashing light. Strobe Light Mode flashes your phone's flashlight rapidly. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and discomfort in others. Do not use Strobe Light Mode if you, or anyone who may see the light, are sensitive to it. Stop immediately if anyone feels unwell. Camera Mode does not flash.
  • Moving machinery. The stroboscopic effect can make a rotating part look slow or stopped while it is turning at full speed. Never touch, adjust or reach towards a part because it looks still. Keep hands, hair, loose clothing and the phone at a safe distance, and follow the machine maker's and your workplace's safety rules.
  • Heat. Running the flashlight or camera for a long time warms the phone. On phones that need it, the App limits strobe speed to protect them, but stop if the phone becomes hot.

3. Readings are estimates

Readings depend on your phone's flashlight and camera, the light, the marking on the part, and on choosing the right match (a part also looks still at fractions and multiples of its true speed). The App is not a certified or calibrated measuring instrument. Do not rely on it alone where an incorrect speed could cause injury, damage or financial loss.
