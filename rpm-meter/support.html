Safety

Strobe Light Mode flashes quickly. If you or anyone nearby has photosensitive epilepsy or is sensitive to flashing light, do not use it. Use Camera Mode, which does not flash.

A part that looks frozen is still turning. Keep hands, hair, loose clothing and the phone clear of moving machinery, and follow your workplace's safety rules. RPM Meter is a measuring aid, not a certified instrument.

Common questions

Why does the mark freeze at more than one speed?

A mark also looks still at whole-number fractions and multiples of the real speed. The true speed is the highest setting that still shows one single mark. Tap ×2 to check: two marks means the reading below was right.

What range can I measure?

Camera Mode reads 30 to 6,000 RPM. Strobe Light goes up to the flash limit of your phone, shown in Flash settings.

Why is my flash limit below 6,000 RPM?

Some phones' flashlight drivers can't pulse that fast without stuttering or stopping. RPM Meter measures what your phone handles and keeps the strobe below it, so the phone doesn't overheat or restart.

The app can't use my camera

Camera Mode needs camera access. Allow it for RPM Meter in your device's settings. Strobe Light Mode uses only the flashlight and works without it.

How do I move readings to a computer?

With Pro, open Machines and tap Export CSV, then send the file by email or save it to your files.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RPM Meter. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RPM Meter. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open Settings in RPM Meter and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy choices. Elsewhere, use your phone's settings: on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads. Pro removes ads entirely.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund page.
