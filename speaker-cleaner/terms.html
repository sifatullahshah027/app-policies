3. What the App can and cannot do

The App plays low-frequency tones that make the speaker cone move, which can help push water drops and loose dust out of the speaker grille. Results depend on your phone, how much water or dust is inside, and how you hold the phone.

  • The App does not repair speakers, dry the inside of a phone, or fix water damage. If sound stays distorted after a few cleans and a day of drying, have the phone checked by its maker or a service centre.
  • The clean score and the speaker test are estimates for comparing before and after. They are not calibrated measurements.
  • The App is not a substitute for your phone maker's instructions for a wet device. Follow those first.

4. Safety and your phone

You use the App at your own risk.

  • Cleaning tones play near full volume. Keep the phone away from your ears and from other people's and animals' ears. Never hold the speaker against your ear while a tone plays.
  • Unplug headphones and turn off Bluetooth audio before a clean. The App stops when it detects headphones, but you should not rely on that alone.
  • Do not charge a wet phone, and do not use the App on a phone that is still dripping, hot, swollen or damaged.
  • Use the strong level and long cleans only as needed. Very long or repeated use at full volume can wear any speaker.
