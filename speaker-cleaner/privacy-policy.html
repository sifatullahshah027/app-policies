Droply: Speaker Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · Applies to Droply on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

Droply plays a low, strong tone through your phone's speaker to help push water and dust out of the speaker grille. It also has a stereo test and a speaker check. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Droply. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers for Droply. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The microphone is used only for the Pro speaker test. The sound is measured on your device for about two seconds. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Your clean history and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google receives your advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interactions. We do not receive that data.
  • You choose whether ads are personalised. See Advertising.
  • Optional Speaker Cleaner Pro is sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. With Pro the app makes no ad requests. See Speaker Cleaner Pro.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.

1. The microphone

Speaker Cleaner Pro includes a speaker test. When you tap Run test, the app plays a short melody and uses the microphone to measure how loud your speaker sounds, so you can compare before and after a clean. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you run the test, using your device's own permission dialog.

  • The app listens only while the test melody plays, for about two seconds, and only after you tap Run test.
  • The sound is turned into one loudness figure (in dB) on your device. Only that figure and the time of the test are kept, on your device, so the next test can show the difference.
  • The sound is never recorded, never saved to a file and never sent anywhere. It is not given to the ad network or to anyone else.

Cleaning, the stereo test and the listen-by-ear check do not use the microphone. If you decline the permission, everything except the speaker test works. You can allow it later in your device's settings.

2. The motion sensor

While the main screen is open, the app reads your phone's tilt sensor so the water droplets on screen slide when you tilt the phone. This happens on your device only. The readings are not stored and not sent anywhere.

4. Advertising

Droply is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by the advertising network listed below, not by us. We do not see or store the data this network collects.

The app shows banner ads on the history, saved-clean and settings screens, small native ads in the history list, full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you return to the main screen, or when you come back to the app after a while), and an optional video ad that you can watch to unlock a Deep Clean or the CSV export. No ad appears during a clean, on the introduction screens or on the Pro offer.

With Pro active the app makes no ad requests and shows no ads. If Pro is already active when you reach the main screen, the tracking prompt and the consent form are not shown either, because no ad network is contacted.

What the ad network collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the network receives:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which it uses to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostic information about the advertising software itself, such as its launch time and errors.

Droply does not give the ad network your microphone sound, your speaker test result, your clean history, your labels and notes, your name, your email address, your photos or your precise location. The app does not ask for your location or your camera.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location.

  • iPhone and iPad: the first time you reach the main screen, Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before the advertising identifier is used. No screen of ours comes before it. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. The app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. No ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time from Droply → Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), sharing data with an ad network for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app, and by the iOS and Android settings described above.

The “Privacy options” row appears in Settings in the regions where these laws apply. In every region, the iOS and Android settings above work.

5. Speaker Cleaner Pro

Droply offers optional Pro access: a weekly subscription, a yearly subscription (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Pro removes all ads and adds the speaker test, the vibration boost, 2-minute Deep Cleans without a video, the custom tone, the Pro sweep and the CSV export without a video. These features work on your device and send nothing anywhere.

  • Who processes the payment: Apple, through the App Store, on iPhone and iPad; Google, through Google Play Billing, on Android. You buy, pay, renew and cancel under your agreement with Apple or Google. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address or store account.
  • What the store shares with the app: which Pro product you hold, whether it is active, and a transaction record that the store uses to confirm the purchase. The app reads this on your device to turn Pro on or off.
  • Nothing is sent to us. There is no server for Droply. The app keeps only whether Pro is active (see section 3). Your Pro status is not given to the ad network.
  • Restore and cancel: use Settings → Restore purchases (or Restore purchases on the Pro screen) with the same store account. To cancel a subscription, tap the Pro card in Settings, or on iPhone and iPad go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and on Android to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
