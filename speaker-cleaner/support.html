Common questions

How do I get the most water out?

Tilt the phone so the speaker faces the floor. Unplug headphones and turn off Bluetooth audio. Hold the big button until it fills, and let the clean run to the end. If the sound is still muffled, run it once more, or switch on “Run twice” under Options. In the app, open Tips for more.

The sound is still muffled after cleaning

Let the phone dry for a few hours, with the speaker facing down, then run another clean. Speaker Cleaner can only move water and loose dust that sit in the grille. If the sound stays distorted after a few cleans and a day of drying, the speaker may be damaged. Have the phone checked by its maker or a service centre.

Is it safe for my phone and my ears?

The tone rises gently over the first second and a half, so there is no sudden blast. It is loud, so keep the phone away from your ears and from babies and pets. Do not charge a wet phone. See the safety notes.

Why is there no Earpiece mode on my iPad or phone?

The Earpiece mode plays the tone through the small call earpiece at the top of the phone. It is available on iPhone and on Android phones that allow it. iPad has no call earpiece, so the mode does not appear there, and some Android phones do not let apps use the earpiece. The main speaker modes work on every device.

Nothing plays, or it says to unplug headphones

The app refuses to play while headphones or Bluetooth audio are connected, so the tone never reaches your ears. Disconnect them and try again. Also check that the volume is up and the phone is not in silent or Do Not Disturb mode.

How does the speaker test work?

With Pro, open Speaker check → Measure → Run test. The microphone listens for about two seconds while a melody plays and shows how loud your speaker is. Test before a clean, then again after it, the same way and in a quiet room, to see the difference in dB. The sound is measured on your phone and never recorded or sent.

The app can't use my microphone

Allow microphone access for Speaker Cleaner in your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apps → Speaker Cleaner → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → Speaker Cleaner → Permissions → Microphone. Only the speaker test needs it.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the app. This row appears in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland. In every region, including the United States, you can use your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Why do I see a video before a Deep Clean or the export?

The app is free and paid for by ads. A 2-minute Deep Clean and the CSV export ask you to watch one short video. If no video can load, you get the feature anyway. Pro users get both without a video.

What does Pro include?

Speaker Cleaner Pro removes all ads and adds the speaker test, the vibration boost, Deep Cleans without a video, the custom tone (60 to 600 Hz), the Pro sweep and the CSV export. You can choose a weekly plan, a yearly plan (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same store account you bought Pro with: the same Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or the same Google account on Android. A purchase on the App Store cannot be restored on Android, or the other way round.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in the store at least 24 hours before the current period (or the free trial) ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Speaker Cleaner → Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Speaker Cleaner → Cancel. When Pro is active, tapping the Pro card in the app's Settings opens the same page. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play help center. We are happy to help by email if something went wrong.

Can I delete my data?

Your clean history is stored only on your device. Delete one clean from its detail screen, clear the whole history from the History screen, or delete the app to remove everything.
