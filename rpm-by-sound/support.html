Common questions

How do I get a good reading?

Hold the phone 20 to 50 cm from the exhaust, the fan hub or the motor. Keep the microphone out of the wind. Keep other engines quiet. Then choose the right mode and the right cylinder or blade count. In the app, open Settings → How to get a good reading.

The RPM is double or half of what I expect

Sound contains harmonics, and sometimes a harmonic is louder than the true tone. Use the harmonic helper on the measuring screen: it shows the half, 1x and 2x candidates, and you tap the one that matches what you hear. Also check the cylinder or blade count.

The app says it is too quiet or uncertain

Move the phone closer to the sound and reduce the background noise. The confidence indicator shows when the reading is steady.

The app can't use my microphone

Allow microphone access for RPM by Sound in your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apps → RPM by Sound → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → RPM by Sound → Permissions → Microphone.

Does the app record me?

No. The sound is analysed on your phone in real time. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How accurate is it?

Readings are estimates. They are good for checking and comparing. They do not replace a calibrated tachometer. In the app, open Settings → Accuracy and limits.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. This row appears in the regions where a privacy law applies. In every region you can also use your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Why do I see a video before the CSV export?

The app is free and paid for by ads. The export asks you to watch one short video. If no video can load, the export works anyway.

Can I delete my data?

Your readings are stored only on your device. Delete one reading, or all of them, in the History screen, or delete the app to remove everything.

How do I move my readings to a new phone?

Export your history as a CSV file from the History screen and keep the file. The app has no account and no cloud copy.

Stay safe

Keep yourself and your phone away from belts, fans, propellers and other moving parts, and away from hot exhausts. Do not run an engine in a closed space.
