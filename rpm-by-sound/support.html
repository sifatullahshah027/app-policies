Common questions

How do I get a good reading?

Hold the phone 20 to 50 cm from the exhaust, the fan hub or the motor. Keep the microphone out of the wind. Keep other engines quiet. Then choose the right mode and the right cylinder or blade count. In the app, open Settings → How to get a good reading.

The RPM is double or half of what I expect

Sound contains harmonics, and sometimes a harmonic is louder than the true tone. Use the harmonic helper on the measuring screen: it shows the half, 1x and 2x candidates, and you tap the one that matches what you hear. Also check the cylinder or blade count.

The app says it is too quiet or uncertain

Move the phone closer to the sound and reduce the background noise. The confidence indicator shows when the reading is steady.

The app can't use my microphone

Allow microphone access for RPM by Sound in your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apps → RPM by Sound → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → RPM by Sound → Permissions → Microphone.

Does the app record me?

No. The sound is analysed on your phone in real time. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How accurate is it?

Readings are estimates. They are good for checking and comparing. They do not replace a calibrated tachometer. In the app, open Settings → Accuracy and limits.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the app. This row appears in the regions where a privacy law applies. In every region you can also use your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Why do I see a video before the CSV export?

The app is free and paid for by ads. The export asks you to watch one short video. If no video can load, the export works anyway. Premium users export without the video.

What does Premium include?

RPM by Sound Premium is an optional weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. It adds PDF reports of your readings, a side-by-side comparison of two readings, and your own dial range and redline for each machine. It also removes all ads and makes the CSV export instant. Measuring, the automatic dial, saving and history are free for everyone. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Where do I find the Premium features?

PDF report: open a saved reading in History and tap PDF report, or in History tap the Select icon (or long-press a reading), pick up to 20 readings and tap the PDF icon. Compare: open a reading and tap Compare with, or select exactly two readings in History. Dial profiles: tap the dial chip under the mode picker on the measuring screen, or open Settings → Dial profiles. If Premium ends, your profiles are kept and the dial goes back to Auto range.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app. Use the same store account you bought Premium with: the same Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or the same Google account on Android. A purchase on the App Store cannot be restored on Android, or the other way round.

How do I cancel Premium?

Cancel in the store at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RPM by Sound → Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RPM by Sound → Cancel. When Premium is active, Settings → Manage in the app opens the same page. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play help center. We are happy to help by email if something went wrong.

Can I delete my data?

Your readings are stored only on your device. Delete one reading, or all of them, in the History screen, or delete the app to remove everything.

How do I move my readings to a new phone?

Export your history as a CSV file from the History screen and keep the file. The app has no account and no cloud copy.

Stay safe

Keep yourself and your phone away from belts, fans, propellers and other moving parts, and away from hot exhausts. Do not run an engine in a closed space.
