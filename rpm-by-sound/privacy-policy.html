RPM by Sound Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Applies to RPM by Sound on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

RPM by Sound is a tachometer app. It listens to an engine, fan or motor through your phone's microphone and works out its speed in RPM. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of RPM by Sound. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers for RPM by Sound. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The microphone sound is analysed on your device. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. Nobody, including us, can listen to it.
  • The readings you save are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google receives your advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interactions. We do not receive that data.
  • You choose whether ads are personalised. See Advertising.
  • Optional Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details; the app checks your subscription on the device. With Premium the app makes no ad requests. See Premium subscriptions.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.

1. The microphone

RPM by Sound needs the microphone to measure. It asks for permission the first time you start a measurement.

  • The app listens only while the measuring screen is open and a measurement is running.
  • The sound is turned into a frequency and an RPM figure on your device, in real time.
  • The sound is never recorded, never saved to a file and never sent anywhere. It is not given to the ad network or to anyone else.

If you decline the microphone permission, the app cannot measure. You can allow it later in your device's settings.

3. Advertising

RPM by Sound is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by the advertising network listed below, not by us. We do not see or store the data this network collects.

The app shows banner ads, small native ads in the history list, full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you return to the measuring screen, or when you come back to the app), and an optional video ad that you can watch to unlock the CSV export. No ad interrupts a measurement: banners and the in-app full-screen ads never appear on the measuring screen, and when a full-screen ad is shown as you come back to the app, the microphone stays paused until you close it.

With Premium active the app makes no ad requests and shows no ads. If Premium is already active when you open the app, the consent form and the tracking prompt are not shown either, because no ad network is contacted.

What the ad network collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the network receives:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which it uses to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostic information about the advertising software itself, such as its launch time and errors.

RPM by Sound does not give the ad network your microphone sound, your saved readings, your labels or notes, your name, your email address, your photos or your precise location. The app does not ask for your location at all.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location.

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for your permission to track, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, before the advertising identifier is used. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. The app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. No ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time from RPM by Sound → Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), sharing data with an ad network for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app, and by the iOS and Android settings described above.

The “Privacy options” row appears in Settings in the regions where these laws apply. In every region, the iOS and Android settings above work.

4. Premium subscriptions

RPM by Sound offers an optional Premium subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly). Premium removes all ads and the video before the CSV export, and adds PDF reports, comparing two readings side by side, and your own dial and redline profiles. These features work on your device and send nothing anywhere. Measuring, the automatic dial, saving, history and the CSV export stay free with or without Premium.

  • Who processes the payment: Apple, through the App Store, on iPhone and iPad; Google, through Google Play Billing, on Android. You buy, pay, renew and cancel under your agreement with Apple or Google. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address or store account.
  • What the store shares with the app: which Premium product you hold, whether it is active, when it renews or expires, and a transaction record that the store uses to confirm the purchase. The app reads this on your device to turn Premium on or off.
  • Nothing is sent to us. There is no server for RPM by Sound. The app keeps only whether Premium is active and when it last checked (see section 2). The subscription status is not given to the ad network.
  • Restore and cancel: use Settings → Restore purchases in the app with the same store account. To cancel, use Settings → Manage in the app, or on iPhone and iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and on Android Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not record, store or upload microphone sound.
  • We do not run a server for RPM by Sound, and we keep no copy of your readings.
  • We do not create accounts or user profiles.
  • We do not use an analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple and Google handle all payments.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. The only sharing for advertising is the sharing with the ad network described in section 3, which you can turn off.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with a privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

  • Your readings are on your device, so you control them directly. Delete one reading, or all of them, in the History screen, or uninstall the app.
  • Ad consent and opt-out: use Settings → Privacy options in the app, and the iOS and Android settings in section 3.
  • Purchase records: held by Apple or Google. Ask them for access or deletion through your store account. We do not hold them.
  • Advertising data held by Google: we do not hold it and cannot look it up. You can manage it at Google My Ad Center and under Google's privacy policy.
  • Anything else: email sifatullahshah27@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Legal basis (GDPR and UK GDPR). Personalised advertising, and the storing or reading of identifiers on your device for advertising, rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent form and can withdraw at any time. Where the law allows it, basic ad delivery, ad measurement and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests. Processing microphone sound and your readings on your device, and checking an active Premium subscription on your device, is needed to provide the app and the subscription you asked for.

10. Children

RPM by Sound is a tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we ask the ad network not to show personalised ads to anyone identified as a child. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your readings are protected by your device's own security. Connections to the ad network and the stores use HTTPS. Purchases are confirmed by Apple or Google. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Keep your device's screen lock on.
