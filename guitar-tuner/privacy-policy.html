Guitar Tuner: Chromatic Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to Guitar Tuner on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

Guitar Tuner is a tuner for string instruments. It listens to your instrument through the microphone and shows the note and how far it is from in tune. It also has a metronome and a pitch pipe that plays a reference note for each string. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Guitar Tuner. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers for Guitar Tuner. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The microphone is used only by the tuner, and only while the Tuner tab is open. The sound is analysed on your device to find the pitch. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Your saved readings, custom tunings and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google receives your advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interactions. We do not receive that data.
  • You choose whether ads are personalised. See Advertising.
  • Optional Guitar Tuner Pro is sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. With Pro the app makes no ad requests. See Guitar Tuner Pro.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.

1. The microphone

The tuner needs the microphone to hear your instrument. The app explains this on a page in its introduction and then asks for permission with your device's own permission dialog. If you skip the introduction page, it asks the first time the tuner starts.

  • The app listens only while the Tuner tab is open and the app is on screen. It stops listening when you switch to the Metronome or Pitch pipe tab, open History or Settings, or leave the app.
  • The sound is analysed in memory on your device to find its pitch: the note, the frequency in Hz and how many cents sharp or flat it is. The sound itself is then discarded.
  • The sound is never recorded, never saved to a file and never sent anywhere. It is not given to the ad network or to anyone else.
  • A reading is kept only when you tap Save, and then only the figures listed in section 2, never the sound.

The metronome and the pitch pipe do not use the microphone. If you decline the permission, the tuner cannot work; the metronome still works, and so does the pitch pipe if you have Pro. You can allow the microphone later in your device's settings.

Guitar Tuner uses no other sensor. It does not use the motion sensors, the camera or your location.

3. Advertising

Guitar Tuner is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by the advertising network listed below, not by us. We do not see or store the data this network collects.

The app shows banner ads on the History, saved-reading and Settings screens, small native ads in the History list, full-screen ads at natural breaks (when you return from History or Settings to the main screen, or when you come back to the app after a while), and an optional video ad that you can watch to unlock the CSV export. No ad appears on the Tuner, Metronome or Pitch pipe tabs, on the introduction screens or on the Pro offer.

With Pro active the app makes no ad requests and shows no ads. If Pro is already active when you first reach the main screen, the tracking prompt and the consent form are not shown either, because no ad network is contacted.

What the ad network collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the network receives:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which it uses to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostic information about the advertising software itself, such as its launch time and errors.

Guitar Tuner does not give the ad network your microphone sound, your saved readings, your custom tunings, your labels and notes, your name, your email address, your photos or your precise location. The app does not ask for your location or your camera.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location.

  • iPhone and iPad: the first time you reach the main screen, Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before the advertising identifier is used. Where a consent form applies, the prompt comes after it. No screen of ours comes before it. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. The app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. No ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time from Guitar Tuner → Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), sharing data with an ad network for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app, and by the iOS and Android settings described above.

The “Privacy options” row appears in Settings in the regions where these laws apply. In every region, the iOS and Android settings above work.

4. Guitar Tuner Pro

Guitar Tuner offers optional Pro access: a weekly subscription, a yearly subscription (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Pro removes all ads and adds the strobe tuner display, all alternate tunings, your own custom tunings, the pitch pipe, extra metronome sounds, subdivisions and per-beat accents, and the CSV export without a video. These features work on your device and send nothing anywhere.

  • Who processes the payment: Apple, through the App Store, on iPhone and iPad; Google, through Google Play Billing, on Android. You buy, pay, renew and cancel under your agreement with Apple or Google. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address or store account. The app uses no other purchase service.
  • What the store shares with the app: which Pro product you hold, whether it is active, and a transaction record that the store uses to confirm the purchase. The app reads this on your device to turn Pro on or off.
  • Nothing is sent to us. There is no server for Guitar Tuner. The app keeps only whether Pro is active (see section 2). Your Pro status is not given to the ad network.
  • Restore and cancel: use Settings → Restore purchases (or Restore purchases on the Pro screen) with the same store account. To cancel a subscription, tap the Pro card in Settings, or on iPhone and iPad go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and on Android to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

  • Your saved readings and custom tunings are on your device, so you control them directly. Delete readings in the History screen and custom tunings in the tuning picker, or uninstall the app.

10. Children

Guitar Tuner is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we ask the ad network not to show personalised ads to anyone identified as a child. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your saved readings and tunings are protected by your device's own security. Connections to the ad network and the stores use HTTPS. Purchases are confirmed by Apple or Google. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Keep your device's screen lock on.
