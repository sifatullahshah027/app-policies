Common questions

The tuner shows nothing, or the note keeps jumping

Pluck one string at a time and mute the others with your hand. Hold the phone close to the instrument. If the tuner still does not react, raise Sensitivity in Settings; in a noisy room, lower it. Also check that microphone access is on (see below).

The tuner reads the wrong string

Tap the string you are tuning to lock it. The tuner then compares against that string only. Tap it again to go back to automatic.

How do I use an alternate tuning or my own tuning?

Open the picker at the top of the Tuner tab and choose your instrument and tuning. Standard tunings are free, and so are Drop D and half-step down for guitar, and the chromatic mode. All other alternate tunings (for example DADGAD, open tunings, Drop C, 7-string, low-G and baritone ukulele) and your own custom tunings are part of Pro.

How do I change the reference pitch (A4)?

Open Settings → Reference pitch (A4). You can set it from 415 to 466 Hz. The usual value is 440 Hz. This is free.

Low bass strings are slow to read

Phone microphones are weak below about 50 Hz, so the lowest strings of a bass or double bass take longer to show. Hold the phone close, pluck firmly and let the string ring. A quiet room helps.

The app can't use my microphone

Allow microphone access for Guitar Tuner in your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apps → Guitar Tuner → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → Guitar Tuner → Permissions → Microphone. Only the tuner needs it. The sound is analysed on your phone and never recorded or sent.

The metronome sounds late over Bluetooth

Bluetooth speakers and headphones add a delay, so the click arrives after the beat shown on screen. The tempo itself stays steady. For playing along in time, use the phone's speaker or wired headphones.

The pitch pipe's low notes are quiet

Small phone speakers cannot play very low notes loudly. Choose the Bright tone for the pitch pipe, or use headphones. Keep the volume moderate with headphones.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language. The app has 11 languages, and can also follow your phone's language.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the app. This row appears in the regions where a privacy law applies. In every region you can also use your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Why do I see a video before the CSV export?

The app is free and paid for by ads. The CSV export of your history asks you to watch one short video. If no video can load, you get the export anyway. Pro users export without a video.

What does Pro include?

Guitar Tuner Pro removes all ads and adds the strobe tuner display, all alternate tunings, your own custom tunings, the pitch pipe, extra metronome sounds, subdivisions and per-beat accents, and the CSV export without a video. You can choose a weekly plan, a yearly plan (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same store account you bought Pro with: the same Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or the same Google account on Android. A purchase on the App Store cannot be restored on Android, or the other way round.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in the store at least 24 hours before the current period (or the free trial) ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Guitar Tuner → Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Guitar Tuner → Cancel. When Pro is active, tapping the Pro card in the app's Settings opens the same page. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play help center. We are happy to help by email if something went wrong.

Can I delete my data?

Your saved readings, custom tunings and settings are stored only on your device. Delete saved readings in the History screen, and custom tunings in the tuning picker. Deleting the app removes everything.
