3. What the App can and cannot do

The App listens to your instrument through the phone's microphone and shows the note it hears, its frequency and how many cents sharp or flat it is. It also has a metronome and a pitch pipe that plays a reference note.

  • Tuner readings are estimates made from the phone's microphone. With a clean pluck they are usually within 1 to 2 cents, but the App is not a calibrated laboratory instrument.
  • Accuracy depends on your phone's microphone, the noise in the room and the instrument itself. Very low notes are harder for a phone microphone to hear.
  • The metronome's timing is sample-accurate in software. Bluetooth speakers and headphones add a delay that the App cannot remove.
  • The App is not meant for professional calibration, or for any use where an exact, certified measurement is required.

4. Safety and your instrument

You use the App at your own risk.

  • Do not over-tighten strings. Tune up slowly, and check that the tuner shows the note and string you expect before you keep turning. A string tuned too high can break and can hurt you or damage the instrument.
  • We are not responsible for broken strings or for damage to an instrument.
  • The pitch pipe plays through the phone's speaker or your headphones. Keep the volume moderate, especially with headphones.
