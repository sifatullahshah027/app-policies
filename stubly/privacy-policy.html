Stubly Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to Stubly on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

Stubly is a pay stub maker for small employers. You enter your business and your employees, and the app calculates and creates the pay stubs you issue to them. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Stubly. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers for Stubly. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Everything you enter, including your business details, your employees and their pay stubs, is stored on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • If you turn on iCloud backup (iPhone and iPad) or Google Drive backup (Android), a copy of that data goes to your own iCloud or Google account. We cannot access it.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics. They contain nothing you type into the app.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID).

1. Data that stays on your device

Stubly works offline. The following is stored only in the app's database on your phone or tablet, and we cannot see it:

  • your employer profiles: business name, address, phone, email, website, tax ID or EIN, country, currency, logo and settings;
  • your employees: name, address, email, phone, employee number, the last four digits of their Social Security or other ID number, pay type, pay rate, default deductions and notes;
  • your pay stubs: pay periods, earnings, taxes, deductions, net pay, year-to-date totals and any signature you draw;
  • your earning and deduction presets, custom fields and templates.

The app never asks for a full Social Security number, bank account or card number.

This data leaves your device only when you choose to send it:

  • Sharing, printing and saving. When you share a pay stub PDF or an employee spreadsheet, print one, or save one to a file, it goes to the app, printer or location you pick. That destination's own privacy terms apply.
  • Backups you create. See section 2.
  • Your device's own backup. The app's database is included in your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) and in Android's backup to your Google account, which Apple or Google run under your account.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data from the device.

2. Backups

Backup to a file

Settings → Data can export everything to a backup file. The file is encrypted on your device with AES-256, using a password you choose. We never see the password or the file. Without the password the file cannot be restored, and we cannot recover it for you.

iCloud backup (iOS)

If you turn it on, the app saves a copy of your Stubly data in its private folder in your iCloud account, including when you leave the app with automatic backup on. Only Stubly on devices signed in to your Apple ID can read that folder. The copy is not encrypted with a password of yours. It is protected by Apple's encryption in transit and in storage, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google Drive backup (Android)

On Android, if you sign in with Google and turn it on, the app saves a copy of your Stubly data in Drive's hidden app data folder in your Google account. It asks Google for one permission only, drive.appdata, which lets Stubly see the files Stubly created in that folder and nothing else in your Drive. The copy is not encrypted with a password of yours. It is protected by Google's encryption in transit and in storage, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Sign-In also gives the app your Google account's basic profile, such as your name and email address. The app uses it only to connect to your Drive and does not send it anywhere. Stubly's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove a cloud backup: in Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps → Stubly → Delete hidden app data. On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) → Stubly → Delete Data. Signing out of Google in Stubly stops future backups.

3. Data we collect

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how it is used. The app logs which screens are opened (an address like /employees/:id, with any record ID removed) and a small set of subscription events: the paywall being shown, a subscription starting, purchases being restored, and Pro being gained or lost. Firebase adds its standard events, such as first open, sessions, app updates and, on iOS, in-app purchase events.

With these events Firebase collects an app-instance ID, a device identifier for this app (the iOS identifier for vendor), device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country, region or city) derived from your IP address. No event contains a name, an amount, an ID number or anything else you type into the app. We do not set a user ID, and the data is not linked to your identity. We use it only to understand and improve the app.

If you are offline, Firebase holds the events on the device and sends them later.

Purchases and subscriptions

Stubly Pro subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card or billing details. The app reads the status of your subscription from the store on your device, and keeps that status on your device. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to your purchase.

4. App lock and device permissions

App lock is off until you turn it on. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint checks are performed by your device's operating system. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives your face or fingerprint data.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run a server for Stubly or keep a copy of your payroll data.
  • We do not show advertising, and we do not use the advertising identifier on either platform. On Android the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not show the iOS tracking prompt because it has nothing to ask it for.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email sifatullahshah27@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Your payroll data is on your device and in your own cloud account, so you control it directly: you can edit or delete any record in the app, export your employees to a spreadsheet, and delete your backups as described in section 2. The usage statistics we hold are not linked to a name or account, so we may be unable to find records that relate to you. We will tell you if that happens.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and improving it for usage statistics. Cloud backup happens only when you turn it on. The payroll data in the app is yours, and you are responsible for having a lawful basis to keep your employees' information.

9. Children

Stubly is a business tool for employers and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security and, if you turn it on, Stubly's app lock. Backup files are encrypted with your password. Connections to Firebase, Google Drive and iCloud use HTTPS. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure. Keep your device's screen lock on and use a strong backup password.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
