Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stubly. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Stubly. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open the paywall in Stubly (Settings → Subscription) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Stubly stores everything on your device, so make a backup first. In Settings → Data, either export an encrypted backup file and keep its password safe, or back up to iCloud (iPhone and iPad) or Google Drive. Install Stubly on the new phone and restore from the same place. Restoring a backup needs Stubly Pro. Creating one is always free.

I forgot my app PIN

On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? and confirm with your phone's own screen lock (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or device passcode). You can then set a new PIN. Your data is not erased.

Are the tax figures official?

Stubly calculates US Social Security and Medicare automatically. You enter other taxes and deductions yourself. Check the figures against your own payroll obligations. Stubly is not tax or legal advice.

Can I delete my data?

Your data lives on your device and in any cloud backup you made. Delete records in the app, or delete the app to remove everything from the device. The privacy policy explains how to delete iCloud and Google Drive backups.
