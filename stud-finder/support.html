How it works

Drywall is screwed or nailed to the studs behind it. Your phone's magnetometer (the compass sensor) notices the small change in the magnetic field near those steel screws and nails. Where it finds a vertical line of them, there is a stud. The app does not see wood itself.

  1. Calibrate. Lay the phone flat on an empty patch of wall, at least a hand's width from corners, outlets and switches, screen facing you. Tap Calibrate here and hold still for a second.
  2. Slide. Move the phone slowly left to right, flat against the wall, about a hand's width every two seconds.
  3. Mark. The app shows Edge as metal comes near and Mark here at the centre, with a vibration and an optional beep. Pencil a tick at the middle of the phone's top edge.
  4. Confirm. Check above or below the mark for the next screw in line. Studs run straight up and down, usually 16 in (40 cm) or 24 in (60 cm) apart.

Calibration and accuracy tips

  • Go slower than feels natural. A screw head is tiny, and a fast sweep can pass it.
  • Remove magnetic cases. Wallet cases, magnetic mounts and metal plates swamp the sensor. Take them off before you calibrate.
  • Recalibrate when the number drifts. If the signal stays high on bare wall, tap Recalibrate on an empty patch. A new wall or room usually needs a new zero.
  • Mind where the sensor sits. On most phones the compass sensor is near the top. Mark at the middle of the top edge, then fine-tune by sliding back across the spot.
  • Use the sensitivity slider. Higher finds smaller or deeper screws but also reacts to more stray metal. Recalibrate after a big change.
  • Keep other metal away. Rings, watches, keys, tools and laptops near the phone change the reading.

Safety

Stud Finder is not a cable or pipe detector. Wires and pipes can trigger it, and a clear reading does not mean a spot is safe. Before drilling near outlets, switches or anywhere wires or pipes may run, turn off the power, use a dedicated cable and pipe detector, or ask an electrician or plumber. See the safety terms.

Common questions

It doesn't find anything

Recalibrate on an empty patch, then slide more slowly, keeping the phone flat. Try a higher sensitivity, or Deep Scan for plaster, tile or thicker walls where screws sit deeper. Some walls have glued drywall or few fasteners, and on some phones the sensor is less sensitive. If nothing shows on any wall, check that your phone has a compass: if the app says “This phone has no magnetometer”, it cannot work on that phone.

The whole wall reads high

Metal studs, corner beads, foil-backed insulation, metal lath behind plaster or a magnetic case can make every spot read high. Remove the case, recalibrate, and lower the sensitivity.

Does it find wood studs?

Only through their screws and nails. The phone has no sensor that sees wood, so a stud with no fastener near the phone stays invisible.

What is Metal Detector mode?

A Pro mode that shows the live field change in microtesla (μT), holds the peak and plays a tone that rises as you get closer to iron or steel. Tap Zero here away from metal first. It does not detect aluminium, copper, brass or gold.

Why do I see a video before Deep Scan or the export?

The app is free and paid for by ads. Without Pro, Deep Scan and the CSV export each offer a choice: tap Watch a short ad, get Pro, or close the sheet. The ad starts only after you tap. Deep Scan then stays on until you close the app; an export is unlocked once. If no video can load, you get the feature anyway.

What does Pro include?

Stud Finder Pro removes all ads and adds Metal Detector mode, Deep Scan and the CSV export without a video. You can choose a yearly plan (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases on the Pro card, or tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same store account you bought Pro with: the same Apple Account on iPhone and iPad, or the same Google account on Android. A purchase on the App Store cannot be restored on Android, or the other way round.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in the store at least 24 hours before the current period (or the free trial) ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stud Finder → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Stud Finder → Cancel.

When Pro is active, tapping Manage subscription on the Pro card in the app's Settings opens the same page. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or the Google Play help center. We are happy to help by email if something went wrong.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Privacy options in the app to reopen the consent form. This row appears in the regions where a privacy law applies, and is hidden while Pro is active. In every region you can also use your device's settings. iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Settings → Language. By default the app follows your phone's language.

Can I delete my data?

Your saved finds are stored only on your device. Delete one find from its detail screen, delete all from the History screen, or delete the app to remove everything. We hold no copy.
