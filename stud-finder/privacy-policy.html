Stud Finder: Wall Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Applies to Stud Finder: Wall Scanner on the Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play (Android)

Stud Finder: Wall Scanner (“Stud Finder”) uses your phone's built-in magnetometer (the compass sensor) to find the metal screws and nails that hold drywall to the studs behind it. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and why. It covers both the iOS app and the Android app, which work the same way. Where they differ, we say so.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of Stud Finder. You can reach us at sifatullahshah27@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and we run no servers for Stud Finder. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The magnetometer readings are processed only on your phone. They are never uploaded and never given to anyone.
  • The finds you save and your settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google receives your advertising identifier, device information, IP address and ad interactions. We do not receive that data.
  • You choose whether ads are personalised. See Advertising.
  • Optional Stud Finder Pro is sold and processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. With Pro the app makes no ad requests. See Stud Finder Pro.
  • The app has no analytics and no crash-reporting service.

1. The magnetometer

While the scan screen or the Metal Detector screen is open, the app reads your phone's magnetometer many times a second. It compares each reading with the value it measured when you tapped Calibrate here (or Zero here) to notice the small change in the magnetic field near a screw or nail.

  • The readings are processed in memory on your device and discarded as you go.
  • They are never sent anywhere: not to us, not to the ad network and not to anyone else.
  • Only the numbers of a find you choose to save are kept, on your device (see section 2).

On iPhone and iPad the magnetometer is a motion sensor, so iOS lists a motion permission text for the app. Reading the magnetometer does not show a permission dialog on iOS or Android. The app does not read your location, and the compass sensor does not reveal it.

3. Advertising

Stud Finder is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by the advertising network listed below, not by us. We do not see or store the data this network collects.

The app shows banner ads on the History, saved-find and Settings screens, small native ads in the History list, full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you return to the scan screen from History or Settings, or when you come back to the app after a while), and an optional video ad that you can choose to watch to use Deep Scan for the rest of the session or to export your finds once. A video ad plays only after you tap Watch a short ad. No ad appears while you scan, on the introduction screens or on the Pro offer.

With Pro active the app makes no ad requests and shows no ads. If Pro is already active when you first reach the scan screen (for example because you bought it on the Pro offer after the introduction), the tracking prompt and the consent form are not shown either, because no ad network is contacted.

What the ad network collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the network receives:

  • The advertising identifier: Apple's IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking) or the Google advertising ID on Android;
  • Device information: model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which it uses to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostic information about the advertising software itself, such as its launch time and errors.

Stud Finder does not give the ad network your magnetometer readings, your saved finds, your labels and notes, your Pro status, your name, your email address, your photos or your precise location. The app does not ask for your location, camera, photos or contacts.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

Ads are personalised (based on your activity across apps and websites) only where you have allowed it. Otherwise you see non-personalised ads, which are based only on the current app and approximate location.

  • iPhone and iPad: when you first reach the scan screen, Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for your permission before the advertising identifier is used. No screen of ours comes before it. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. The app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent-management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related processing. No ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change your choice at any time from Stud Finder → Settings → Privacy options.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and the laws of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other states), sharing data with an ad network for targeted advertising may count as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the app, and by the iOS and Android settings described above.

The “Privacy options” row appears in Settings in the regions where these laws apply, and is hidden while Pro is active because no ads are shown. In every region, the iOS and Android settings above work.

4. Stud Finder Pro

Stud Finder offers optional Pro access: a yearly subscription (which starts with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Pro removes all ads and adds Metal Detector mode, Deep Scan and the CSV export without a video ad. These features work on your device and send nothing anywhere.

  • Who processes the payment: Apple, through the App Store, on iPhone and iPad; Google, through Google Play Billing, on Android. You buy, pay, renew and cancel under your agreement with Apple or Google. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address or store account.
  • What the store shares with the app: which Pro product you hold, whether it is active, and a transaction record that the store uses to confirm the purchase. The app checks this with the store on your device to turn Pro on or off. No third-party purchase service is used.
  • Nothing is sent to us. There is no server for Stud Finder. The app keeps only whether Pro is active (see section 2). Your Pro status is not given to the ad network.
  • Restore and cancel: use Restore purchases on the Pro card in Settings, or on the Pro screen, with the same store account. To cancel a subscription, tap Manage subscription on the Pro card in Settings, or on iPhone and iPad go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and on Android to Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We do not upload or store magnetometer readings anywhere but in the finds you save on your device.

  • We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple and Google handle all payments.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. The only sharing for advertising is the sharing with the ad network described in section 3, which you can turn off.
